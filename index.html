II · The chronicle

Lore

A childhood shaped by two inheritances.

Aria grew up in Abergavenny, Wales, between her maternal Veela charm and the colder, nocturnal traits of her father’s ancient vampire aristocratic clan.

At a young age, the manifestation of her magic could become unbalanced. When her emotions were disturbed, her Veela charm could shift into a cold aura that seemed to freeze the shadows nearby.

Her parents raised her privately, seeking to conceal her dual heritage from the local magical society. As a child, Aria often felt isolated; she had to suppress and balance instincts that seemed to pull in opposite directions.

That chapter changed when her Hogwarts acceptance letter arrived at the age of eleven.
